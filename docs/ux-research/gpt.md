# GPT — NEXUS SELECT benchmark opinion (2026-11-12)
Status: preliminary opinion, not three-agent consensus. Current Local live site has not been independently operated by GPT; known defects are from Muse's report. Do not interpret this as a verified comparative score.

## Comparison
- SSENSE (https://www.ssense.com/): reference for minimalist product detail, white square imagery, desktop sticky information. NEXUS has adopted this direction; actual mobile gallery still needs single-image and selected-color-only fixes.
- Kith (https://kith.com/): candidate reference for editorial home and streetwear identity. NEXUS should strengthen its own visual identity rather than copy imagery.
- END. (https://www.endclothing.com/): reference candidate for discovery, filters and product lists. NEXUS should test brand/category/size availability filtering and mobile navigation.
- Slam Jam (https://slamjam.com/): candidate reference for streetwear multi-brand presentation.
- BEAMS (https://www.beams.co.jp/): candidate for MEN/WOMEN/KIDS and brand/category navigation.
- atmos (https://www.atmos-tokyo.com/): candidate for sneaker launches and color presentation.

## NEXUS assessment
Design direction is coherent, but visual parity with leading shops is not yet demonstrated. Known mobile gallery and home brand-index defects must be fixed. Product discovery and purchase confidence need actual interaction testing. Overseas sourcing, inspection and two-leg shipment progress could be a differentiator once fully verified; do not claim it is superior before comparison. Overseas-exclusive and Japan-sold-out inventory is a potential merchandising advantage.

## Priorities
1. Correct known usability defects and regressions before visual polish.
2. Compare the same tasks on desktop/mobile: find a brand/product, filter size, switch color, inspect photos, add to cart, understand shipping/returns, track order where accessible.
3. Record actual evidence, exact URLs, viewport and limitations; compare benefits and implementation effort. No invented scores.
4. Muse and Grok: publish independent opinions in docs/ux-research/muse.md and grok.md, read each other's reports, challenge GPT's assumptions, then consolidate agreement/disagreement in consensus.md. Do not overwrite others' reports. No user relay. Research only; no production changes.
