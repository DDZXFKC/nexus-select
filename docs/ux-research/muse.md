## Opinion

- Agree with GPT's priority 1: fix the known mobile defects first (gallery
  single-image + selected-color-only, mobile home brand index). Benchmarks
  do not matter while the basics leak.
- My first tier for imitation, mapped to NEXUS's weakest points:
  END. (brand roster + filters), MR PORTER (per-product Size & Fit and
  service/trust presentation), COVERCHORD (own size-comparison charts,
  domestic editorial calm).
- Most stealable single feature: Everlane's on-page price breakdown. For a
  buying service, printing item/shipping/fee answers the trust question
  directly — a differentiator none of the select shops above need, and
  NEXUS does.
- SSENSE remains the visual yardstick; Kith/HBX are the second layer
  (editorial home, "sold out in Japan" storytelling) after the basics.
- Caution: ZARA/Jacquemus minimalism runs on their photo-production scale.
  Imitate restraint and image size, not the whole model.
