# Apparel reference sites — three-way research (2026-11-12)

Owner instruction: find apparel e-commerce sites (Japan or overseas) that are
worth referencing / imitating for NEXUS SELECT, researched by Muse, ChatGPT
and Grok, and share the results here on GitHub.

Context for all researchers: NEXUS SELECT is a Japan-based select/buying shop
(sneakers + apparel, items not buyable in Japan, sourced mainly from the US).
Design baseline already chosen by the owner: SSENSE-style minimal — white,
large product imagery stacked vertically, very little decoration. Hybrid
model: listed products for direct sale + a "buying request" flow for
unlisted items. Current known weaknesses to find answers for: mobile product
gallery (color separation, swipe behaviour), mobile brand index, size-guide
presentation, trust cues for high-price proxy purchases.

Format per site: name — country — URL — what to steal (one or two lines) —
which part of NEXUS SELECT it maps to. Add your section, do not overwrite
others. Muse consolidates after all three are in.

## 1. Muse's list

- SSENSE — Canada — https://www.ssense.com — the adopted baseline: extreme
  minimal, huge vertical product imagery, sparse text. (Already the product
  page reference; keep as the yardstick for every other candidate.)
- END. — UK — https://www.endclothing.com — launches/drawings (raffle) UX,
  brand roster presentation, clean grids that stay readable at 800+ brands.
  Maps to: NEW ARRIVALS / launch-style drops, brand index.
- Kith — US — https://www.kith.com — editorial lookbooks fused with commerce;
  collaboration storytelling that sells the product as a world, not an SKU.
  Maps to: home editorial, product story blocks.
- HBX — Hong Kong — https://www.hbx.com — media (Hypebeast) + commerce, and
  an archive section selling rare past items. Maps to: "sold out in Japan"
  sourcing story, content-led discovery.
- MR PORTER — UK — https://www.mrporter.com — per-product Size & Fit notes
  and calm luxury service cues (packaging, delivery, returns presented as
  quality). Maps to: size guides, trust on high-price items.
- Farfetch — UK/global — URL to confirm at consolidation — marketplace of
  boutiques presented as one coherent store. Maps to: the buying-service
  model itself (distributed sourcing, single storefront).
- Très Bien — Sweden — URL to confirm — small, confident curation; nothing
  on the page that does not earn its place. Maps to: overall restraint.
- Slam Jam — Italy — https://www.slamjam.com — culture-first select shop,
  raffles, community tone without visual noise. Maps to: brand voice.
- COS — Sweden/UK — URL to confirm — architectural minimalism, disciplined
  whitespace and typography. Maps to: home/category composition.
- Jacquemus — France — URL to confirm — social-first playful minimalism,
  oversized single-product imagery. Maps to: hero imagery, campaign feel.
- ZARA — Spain — URL to confirm — image-led to the extreme; the UI almost
  disappears. Maps to: how far minimal can go on category pages.
- Lemaire — France — URL to confirm — quiet restraint, garment-detail pages
  that let construction speak. Maps to: apparel product pages.
- ETQ Amsterdam — Netherlands — URL to confirm — slow, detailed product
  examination (materials, close-ups). Maps to: footwear product pages.
- Everlane — US — URL to confirm — radical price transparency: the cost
  breakdown printed on the product page. Maps to: showing the buying-price
  breakdown (item + shipping + fee) as a trust feature.
- GR8 — Japan — http://gr8.jp — Tokyo street/luxury select; brand-mix
  presentation closest to NEXUS's assortment at home. Maps to: brand index,
  domestic benchmark.
- NUBIAN — Japan — URL to confirm — leading Japanese street select EC;
  domestic operations benchmark (delivery, campaigns, LINE/social linkage).
- COVERCHORD — Japan — https://coverchord.com — editorial select shop with
  its own per-product size-comparison charts and unhurried curation.
  Maps to: size-guide presentation, editorial product pages.
- 1LDK — Japan — URL to confirm — lifestyle-minimal select aesthetic;
  clothing presented as part of daily life. Maps to: home warmth (the cafe
  side of the owner's taste), apparel imagery.
